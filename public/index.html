Climate, with sources shown

Useful context, without pretending to predict a harvest.

The explorer uses NASA POWER daily rainfall and temperature for a selected point. It compares the climate record with crop and farmer priorities using explicit rules. It does not calculate field-level yield, soil moisture, prices, or irrigation demand.

Scores are a starting point for discussion. Check them against field observations, local soil tests, and advice from people who know the area. NASA provides the weather data and does not endorse the rotation scores.

Before you use a score

Common questions

Does Field Shift predict crop yield?

No. It compares weather context and rotation characteristics. It does not estimate harvest yield or provide yield benchmarks.

Does it replace an agronomist or extension worker?

No. Use the comparison as a conversation aid and check it with field observations, soil tests, and local agronomic advice.

Where is field information stored?

On fieldshift.cloud, saved field records and cached climate files are stored on the server that runs the app. The app has no user account system. Do not enter confidential personal information. See the privacy notice.

What information is sent to data providers?

NASA POWER receives the selected field coordinates and climate date range. OpenStreetMap Nominatim may receive the place-search text or coordinates used to name a map pin.
