# Deliverables & Judging Tips

## Must-Have Deliverables
- Working prototype (even if limited to demo locations)
- 3–5 minute demo video showing full user flow
- Clear project description on the Space Apps platform covering:
  - NASA datasets used
  - How the three rotations are compared
  - Farmer-centered design choices
  - Limitations and disclaimer

## What Judges Look For
- Actual use of NASA open data
- Comparison of at least three rotation strategies with evidence
- Interpretability for non-technical users
- Clear explanation of benefits, risks, and data gaps
- Emphasis that the tool supports (does not replace) local expertise

## Quick Wins
- Clean, readable UI
- Charts that directly support the recommendations
- Strong disclaimer
- Reference to specific data values in the “Why this?” text
